**The Developer plan is Managed Postgres for side projects, prototypes and small apps. You get a highly available, two-node Postgres cluster with daily backups for \$4 a month.**

## What the Developer plan includes

- **High availability:** a primary and a replica, each on a `shared-cpu-1x` Machine with 256MB of memory, with automatic failover.
- **Daily backups:** one full backup a day.
- **Storage:** 1GB to start, growing automatically up to 5GB.
- **Up to 20 connections**, straight to Postgres.
- **Auto-pause:** the cluster pauses when nothing is connected and wakes on the next connection. See [Auto-pause](#auto-pause).
- Everything else in [Managed Postgres](/postgres): encryption, [extensions](/postgres/extensions), [metrics](/postgres/metrics), and access from your organization's [private network](/networking/private-networking).

## Pricing

The Developer plan costs **\$4 a month**, including storage. You pay for the whole month, including the time the cluster is paused.

The Developer plan is included in the [Free plan](/about/free-plan). A Free plan organization can have one Developer plan cluster, and its cost counts toward the \$5 of monthly usage.

## How it differs from other plans

The Developer plan is smaller and simpler than the other [Managed Postgres plans](/postgres#pricing):

| | Developer | Basic and up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memory per node | 256MB | 1GB and up |
| Connection pooling (PgBouncer) | No. Clients connect to Postgres directly | Yes |
| Connections | Up to 20 | Depends on plan |
| Storage | Up to 5GB | Up to 1TB |
| Storage cost | Included in the \$4 | \$0.28/GB a month |
| Restore | From a backup only | From a backup or a point in time |
| Postgres settings | Fixed | Can be changed |
| Maintenance | Any time | In a [weekly window](/postgres/maintenance-windows) you choose |
| Auto-pause | Yes | No |
| Support | No direct support contact. We still respond to alerts on your cluster | Direct support from Fly.io |

## Maintenance

Developer plan clusters don't have a maintenance window. We can run maintenance at any time. We work to avoid downtime, but your app may see short interruptions, so make sure it reconnects when a connection drops.

## Auto-pause

A Developer plan cluster pauses after 15 minutes with no client connections. While it's paused, the dashboard shows that it wakes on the next connection.

When a client connects, the cluster wakes. This usually takes about 15 seconds, and can take up to 25. The connection waits for the cluster to wake, so set your client's connection timeout to at least 30 seconds.

## Storage limit

Your cluster starts with 1GB of storage and grows automatically, up to 5GB.

When your databases get close to 5GB, we email you and schedule an upgrade to the Basic plan. You can cancel the upgrade. If you cancel it and your data reaches 5GB, Postgres runs out of space and stops accepting writes.

## Create a Developer plan cluster

```bash
fly mpg create --plan dev-256
```

Your cluster gets a 1GB volume by default.

## Connect to your cluster

Use the `DATABASE_URL` that [`fly mpg attach`](/postgres/create-and-connect) sets on your app. Its hostname starts with `pgbouncer`, like on other plans, but on the Developer plan it connects straight to Postgres. You don't need to change it if you upgrade to a plan with PgBouncer.

You don't need PgBouncer settings, such as disabling prepared statements for transaction mode. Keep your app's connection pool small, since all your clients share the 20 connections.

## Upgrade to a larger plan

You can move a Developer plan cluster to Basic or a larger plan at any time from the dashboard. Your disk grows to the new plan's default size, 10GB on Basic. You can't move a cluster from another plan to the Developer plan.

On the [Free plan](/about/free-plan), upgrade your organization to Pay As You Go before you move the cluster to a larger plan.

See [Changing your MPG plan](/postgres/cluster-configuration#changing-your-mpg-plan).
