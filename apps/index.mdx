An app on Fly.io can be anything from a simple frontend web app to a complex arrangement of processes and Machines all doing their own thing. Check out the [Fly Apps overview](/apps/overview) for details.

## Fly Launch

_If you're looking to deploy an app on Fly.io, you probably want Fly Launch. Use Fly Launch to create your app and then manage the whole lifecycle, from starting to scaling to changing and redeploying._

- **[Get started](/getting-started):** Launch your own app or try a demo app.

- **[Use Fly Launch](/launch/index):** Learn how to create, configure, deploy, and scale your app.
