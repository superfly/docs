**Fly.io doesn't have a free tier, but if your monthly bill comes to less than \$5, we waive it. This page explains how the waiver works and who gets it.**

## How the waiver works

If your organization's bill for the month is under \$5, you don't pay it. If it's \$5 or more, you pay the full amount.

For example:

- A \$4.20 bill is waived, so you pay \$0.
- A \$7.00 bill isn't waived, so you pay \$7.00. The first \$5 isn't taken off.

All usage counts toward the bill, including Machines, Sprites, Managed Postgres, volumes and data transfer.

## Who gets the waiver

The waiver applies to Pay As You Go organizations with a credit card on file. You don't need to sign up for it.

Billing is monthly per organization, so each organization's bill is checked on its own. For details, see [How Billing Works](/about/billing).

## How it fits with the free trial

New accounts start with a [free trial](/about/free-trial): \$1 of credit, with no credit card required. The waiver starts after you add a credit card.

1. **Before you add a card:** you use the \$1 trial credit, with [trial limits](/about/free-trial#trial-limits).
2. **After you add a card:** the trial limits are lifted, and any month that comes to less than \$5 is waived.

## What fits under \$5 a month

A small app can stay under \$5. The cheapest always-on app is a `shared-cpu-1x` Machine with 256MB of RAM in Ashburn (`iad`) or Secaucus (`ewr`). It costs \$2.19 per 30 days of continuous runtime, plus any outbound data transfer.

Machines that [stop when idle](/launch/autostop-autostart) only cost compute while they run, which helps keep a side project under \$5.

To estimate your bill, use the [pricing calculator](https://fly.io/calculator). For all prices, see [Fly.io Resource Pricing](/about/pricing).

## FAQ

### Does Fly.io have a free tier?

No. There's no free tier or monthly free usage allowance. Instead, new accounts get a \$1 [free trial](/about/free-trial), and monthly bills under \$5 are waived.

### Is the first \$5 of every bill free?

No. Only bills under \$5 are waived. A bill of \$5 or more is charged in full.

### Do I need a credit card?

Yes. The waiver applies once your organization has a credit card on file. Without one, you can only use the \$1 trial credit.

### Do Sprites and Managed Postgres count toward the \$5?

Yes. All usage on your bill counts, including Machines, Sprites and Managed Postgres.

### Does the waiver cap my bill?

No. If your usage goes over \$5, you pay for all of it. To keep track of what you're spending, see [Cost Management](/about/cost-management).
