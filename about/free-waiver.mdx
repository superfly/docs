**If your personal organization's monthly bill comes to less than \$5, we waive it. The waiver is for existing organizations. New organizations start on the [Free plan](/about/free-plan). This page explains how the waiver works and who still gets it.**

<Note>
New organizations don't get the waiver. Your personal organization can use the [Free plan](/about/free-plan) instead, with \$5 of usage every month.
</Note>

## How the waiver works

If your personal organization's bill for the month is under \$5, you don't pay it. If it's \$5 or more, you pay the full amount.

For example:

- A \$4.20 bill is waived, so you pay \$0.
- A \$7.00 bill isn't waived, so you pay \$7.00. The first \$5 isn't taken off.

All usage counts toward the bill, including Machines, Sprites, Managed Postgres, volumes and data transfer.

## Who gets the waiver

The waiver applies to your existing personal organization, the one we create when you sign up. It needs to be on Pay As You Go billing with a credit card on file. You don't need to sign up for it.

Organizations on the Free plan don't get the waiver. Existing organizations keep it until they move to the new plans. We'll tell you before that happens.

Other organizations you create or join don't get the waiver, so their bills are charged in full.

## What fits under \$5 a month

A small app can stay under \$5. The cheapest always-on app is a `shared-cpu-1x` Machine with 256MB of RAM in Ashburn (`iad`) or Secaucus (`ewr`). It costs \$2.19 per 30 days of continuous runtime, plus any outbound data transfer.

Machines that [stop when idle](/launch/autostop-autostart) only cost compute while they run, which helps keep a side project under \$5.

To estimate your bill, use the [pricing calculator](https://fly.io/calculator). For all prices, see [Fly.io Resource Pricing](/about/pricing).

## FAQ

### Does Fly.io have a free tier?

Yes. The [Free plan](/about/free-plan) gives your personal organization \$5 of usage every month.

### Do new organizations get the waiver?

No. New organizations start on the [Free plan](/about/free-plan) instead.

### Is the first \$5 of every bill free?

No. Only bills under \$5 are waived. A bill of \$5 or more is charged in full.

### Do I need a credit card?

Yes. The waiver applies once your personal organization has a credit card on file and is on Pay As You Go.

### Does the waiver apply to all my organizations?

No. Only your personal organization gets the waiver. Other organizations you create or join are billed in full.

### Do Sprites and Managed Postgres count toward the \$5?

Yes. All usage on your bill counts, including Machines, Sprites and Managed Postgres.

### Does the waiver cap my bill?

No. If your usage goes over \$5, you pay for all of it. To keep track of what you're spending, see [Cost Management](/about/cost-management).
